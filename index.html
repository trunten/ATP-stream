Independent personal-use utility

A home for the live moments.

ATP Recorder is a small personal project designed to monitor the Accidental Tech Podcast livestream, capture the audio when a broadcast is live, and save the finished recording in the owner's Google Drive.

How Google Drive data is used ↗ View the project on GitHub ↗

The idea

Designed to run quietly in the background.

One job: keep a personal archive of the live broadcast, without needing to stay awake or leave a laptop switched on.

01

Detect the livestream

Periodically checks ATP's public livestream status to see whether audio is available.

02

Capture the audio

When configured and authorised, a background recorder captures the live audio for later listening.

03

Archive to Google Drive

Saves recordings to a folder selected by the owner, so they can be played on a phone or tablet.

Google Drive access

Permission for one practical purpose.

Google authorisation is used by the recorder to create and upload files in the owner's chosen Drive folder. The underlying rclone connection may request broad Google Drive permissions even though the recorder is configured to use only that folder. Google credentials are not entered on this website.

Private by design, personal by purpose.

This is an informational website, not a sign-in page. The recorder is intended for the owner's use, is not a publicly available recording service, and does not sell or advertise using Google account data. Read the privacy policy for details.
